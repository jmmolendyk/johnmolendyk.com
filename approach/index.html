Approach

Optimize then automate. Build governance in so teams can move faster.

The principle came from a Six Sigma engagement over twenty years ago and still holds for AI: fix the process, then add the technology, and keep controls proportionate to the risk so teams can adopt AI with confidence.

  1. Define the problem and the measure

    Start by identifying the problem or opportunity. Understand what it is, what solving it means for the business, and how we’ll measure success once it’s in place.

  2. Optimize, then automate

    Fix the process first, then build the solution around the new needs and the technology available, including AI. Responsibility is built in from the start.

  3. Measure and improve

    Track the new process against the measures we set at the start, and make iterative changes to keep improving its performance.

  4. Hand over ownership

    Transfer the solution to its owner with the tools needed to maintain ownership, including documentation, training and a clear view of how it’s performing.

Governance as enabler

Good governance is how teams say yes to AI with confidence. Lightweight, proportionate controls speed adoption because people know where the lines are.

Design to support adoption

Human-centered design drives adoption by making expectations clear to everyone: disclosures people can read, workflows that feel intuitive and controls that fit the context.

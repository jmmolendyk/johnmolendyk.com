About

Governance by someone who has built and run the systems

I'm an independent AI governance consultant and the founder of Flowhaus.ai. For more than twenty years I have moved loosely defined business problems from discovery to controlled deployment and sustainable operations, working as a developer, business analyst, project manager, product owner and researcher in financial services, healthcare and technology.

That history is why I approach AI governance as an operating problem: audits, vendors, migrations, regulatory responses and rules-based decision systems all taught me what makes controls stick. Most recently I was a Senior UX Researcher at Meta, partnering with Privacy, Legal and Compliance on AI and voice features.

Case study

Digitizing the customer experience: from mailed paper surveys to a scalable feedback platform

Customer feedback depended on a 53-question paper survey mailed after the appointment, and the requests inside it moved through spreadsheets and email. We digitized it: a customer experience platform built on the Qualtrics API and Salesforce data, giving teams near real-time access to feedback.

The situation

A 53-question paper survey was mailed to participants after their appointment and arrived up to two weeks after service. Research found that over 70% of contact requests went unaddressed, lost to illegibility or timing. Teams tracked requests across email, spreadsheets and phone calls, staff spent up to 18 minutes logging a single request across several systems, and there was no scalable way to manage branding or devices for 1,000+ sites. The inconsistency also created compliance risk.

My role

As interim Product Owner across 15+ applications, I led the digital transformation, working with engineering, sales and operations through a phased approach.

What I did

  • Mapped before building. Interviews and shadowing across sales, customer experience and regional operations, then a sitemap of the full survey lifecycle that defined roles, data sources and system responsibilities.
  • Digitized the survey end to end. Built the platform with an API integration to Qualtrics and Salesforce data for near real-time access to customer feedback, with hardware and training delivered to client sites and technical administration of the Qualtrics survey platform.
  • Piloted first. A desktop pilot for program and operations admins proved the enrollment and request flows before the tablet and phone version for field teams.
  • Standardized the workflow. A platform for customer communications and hardware replacement requests, with device replacement tickets and history, contact requests that minimize free text, and API-driven enrollment of clients and sites with branding and metadata. Multiple teams moved off spreadsheets and email.

“John has a great attention to detail and a unique ability to make complex topics easy to understand.”

Senior Manager, Project Development (ex-United Health Group)

Outcome

Time to document and manage a request dropped 64%, saving 13 minutes each, and every contact request now reached operations within 24 hours of service. Sites got self-service branded surveys, and survey data was shared across CX, operations, sales and compliance.

Insight

An AI-powered news and research platform designed to make it easier to follow developments in AI governance, understand emerging trends and turn information into informed perspectives.

Personal buildAI governanceArticlesKnowledge center

2,500+ articles from more than 290 sources

What it is

Insight collects articles and organizes them into common-sense themes. It highlights specific sources and brings forward the articles most relevant to what I’m working on now.

A daily briefing gives me a quick snapshot, where I can expand the categories I care about and collapse the rest.

I can search the full library of collected articles and, from within any article, start developing ideas for my own writing while the tool surfaces other articles and sources worth considering.

I can also manage my sources and create new categories for the article taxonomy.

Why I built it

Staying current in a field as fast-moving as AI governance is nearly impossible, and the pace keeps increasing. New threats, technologies, solutions, regulations, standards and practices emerge constantly across multiple disciplines.

I built Insight to keep me focused on information from core sources on a regular basis, while exposing me to new sources without inundating me.

It goes beyond a list of bookmarks or a spreadsheet. AI parses newsletters and traverses my sources for me, so I get a consolidated perspective quickly and know there is one place where the material I care about most lives.

I can add individual articles or sources, or drop in a newsletter and have the articles and new sources collected. I can also see how each source performs and decide whether it is meeting my needs or should be removed.

Approach

Insight was developed through an iterative, AI-assisted process. I defined the product requirements, information architecture, taxonomy, collection workflow and editorial experience, and used AI coding tools to help implement and refine the application.

The design emphasizes source quality, relevance and traceability. Rather than treating AI-generated summaries as authoritative, the platform supports research and judgment while preserving access to the original sources.

The project continues to evolve as I explore more effective ways to use AI for information management, research and content development.

Planned updates

Quality review and update

Now that I have collected enough articles and sources, I’ll work with AI to assess the quality of both. Some of the early collection was incomplete, though the articles themselves are relevant, so rebuilding the content around each article is key to keeping it searchable and relevant.

Personalization

I want to tell Insight what I’m currently working on and focused on, so my daily briefing is as relevant as possible.

Diverse storage

A home for white papers, frameworks, standards and other knowledge beyond articles that I want considered in my own writing. That includes documents I create myself and my own published articles, kept for reference in future work.

Writing center

Grounding with RAG in my voice and writing style, so suggested drafts start closer to how I write. I also plan more control over article creation: length, variability, topics, number of sources, number and placement of quotes, citations in a couple of useful formats, and multiple versions when needed.
